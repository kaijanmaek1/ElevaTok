Privacy & how it works

ElevaTok is a local, mood-guided search assistant. This browser demo opens directly, with no extension installation. Choose a mood and an experience, optionally add a note, then click Open TikTok. Videos and results appear only on TikTok.

Your consent and control

A consent dialog appears whenever the browser demo loads. Consent is not remembered. Personalized searches and loading saved search preferences stay paused until you choose Agree and continue. Decline or Escape leaves the introduction available. Consent & data choices lets you pause personalization again. Delete stored data removes saved feedback, known older-version data, the saved theme and your current check-in; it does not accept consent. Declining alone does not erase existing preferences. Display preferences and the technical launcher tab identifier operate separately. Switching directly back from TikTok to the already-open tab keeps feedback available.

Your check-in

The raw note and mood label stay in page memory. They are not saved in browser storage or sent to a server. Closing or reloading the ElevaTok page clears them. Ordinary input is matched locally against supported categories and specific subjects. Complex or unsupported wording can be misunderstood.

Opening TikTok

Only a locally generated phrase containing recognized topic and subject words is placed in the TikTok search URL when you click. The raw note is never copied into it. The phrase is not previewed in ElevaTok; it is naturally visible in TikTok's search box, address bar and browser history. Topic words can still reveal interests. TikTok receives the query and the normal information involved in visiting its site, and applies its own data practices.

Appearance

Light and Dark controls change the interface appearance. Your explicit choice is saved locally as elevatokTheme so it is remembered after a browser restart. Only the display preference is stored there, never a mood, note, search or feedback. Without a saved choice, the interface starts with your system color preference. Fonts use a local sans-serif system stack; no font service is contacted.

Optional feeling check-ins and everyday tips

Choose an overall feeling from 1 (very low) to 5 (very good) before opening TikTok. Return to the same ElevaTok tab to enter an after rating or skip. Improvement gives the latest searched topic, subject and phrase a small positive preference adjustment; worsening gives a negative adjustment; no change does not adjust weights. Ratings are never sent to TikTok or stored: only derived preference weights are kept in session storage after consent. The before rating and pending search exist only in the open page and are replaced by a new search, or cleared by completing, skipping, declining, deleting or closing the page. No longitudinal mood record is created. These self-reports cannot establish that content caused a change. Optional tips are general everyday ideas based on selected headspace, drawing on NHS wellbeing guidance; they are not treatment or a substitute for your care plan.

One-tap feedback

More like this and Less like this apply to the last search opened from ElevaTok, not to a particular TikTok video. Feedback is optional. It adjusts preferences for derived topics, subjects and phrase variants. Explicit new interests and exclusions still take priority. More like this can prepare a follow-up on the last supported subject when the new note is empty; changing the mood, experience or note chooses a new direction. Undo reverses the last vote until another search is opened.

Extension tab-session storage holds a rotation number, derived preference weights, recent choice identifiers, the last search's normalized topic/subject/choice identifier and vote, and a temporary Undo snapshot. It does not hold the full note, mood label, search URL or actual TikTok browsing history. The extension cannot observe videos watched, likes, comments, Favorites or TikTok search results. Recent choice identifiers refer only to ElevaTok's own preset search templates.

Retention and reset

These preferences last for the tab session, including between view openings. They clear when that extension session ends, such as closing the tab. Reset feedback and delete previous data clears them immediately and removes known data keys from earlier QuickTok/MoodLoop/demo versions. No old profile is imported. The current raw note stays on the page until you close or reload it.

Browser demo and hosting

This public demo does not need extension permissions. Derived preferences use this tab's session storage and clear when the tab session ends. Theme choice uses local storage. There is no analytics code or backend for check-in data. The hosting provider receives normal website request information. TikTok receives generated searches only when you choose to open it. Closing or reloading clears raw check-in inputs. Reset removes derived preferences; Delete stored data also clears the current check-in and theme preference.

Limits

TikTok selects and ranks results, so different phrases may return overlapping videos. Exclusions guide suggested topics but cannot remove results from TikTok. ElevaTok does not change For You recommendations, create saved collections or automate account actions. It is an independent prototype, not affiliated with TikTok, medical care or a legal compliance certification.
